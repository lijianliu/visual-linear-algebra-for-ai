Gradient Visualizer for Neural Network Intuition — This tool plots mathematical functions and computes their derivative (gradient) numerically at any point. In neural networks, gradients are the foundation of learning: during backpropagation, the network computes gradients to understand how to adjust weights to minimize error. The yellow tangent line shows the gradient's direction and steepness — gradient descent follows this slope downhill to find optimal solutions.
